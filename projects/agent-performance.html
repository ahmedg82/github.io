Agent & Showroom Performance Tracker

A scorecard bringing dynamic rankings, performance tiers and regional access into one reporting experience.

My role
Power BI scorecards and regional reporting
Tools
Power BI · DAX · Power BI Service · RLS

The business question

Which agents need attention, and how does performance compare?

Regional managers need to compare performance within their area and identify where to follow up. This requires both clear scorecards and access scoped to their responsibilities.

The approach

  1. Use DAX to support dynamic rankings and performance tiers.
  2. Present agent performance through a dedicated Power BI scorecard.
  3. Publish through Power BI Service with row-level security for regional access.

What the work enables

The tracker gives regional managers self-service access to agent performance information. Rankings and tiers support a more focused review of the team.

This overview draws on the project experience documented in my CV. The preview illustrates the reporting concept without reproducing business data.

Sales Performance Command Center

A reporting suite connecting sales, targets, regions and product mix so commercial teams can explore performance from a shared view.

My role
Dashboard design, data modelling and commercial analytics
Tools
Power BI · DAX · SQL Server · SAP SD

The business question

Where are we performing well, and where should we focus next?

Commercial reporting needs to connect the headline sales result with the regions, products and agents behind it. A single total cannot explain where attention is needed.

The approach

  1. Bring SAP SD and SQL Server data into a multi-layer Power BI reporting suite.
  2. Organize analysis around YTD versus target, regional comparisons, agent scorecards, product mix and discounts.
  3. Use row-level security to give regional managers access to the data relevant to their role.

What the work enables

The resulting suite supports regional and executive performance reviews through a common reporting structure. It brings the headline result and its commercial detail into the same analytical workflow.
